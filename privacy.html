Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Information Collected: When connecting a TikTok account via OAuth 2.0, this application stores encrypted access/refresh tokens and basic profile info (open_id, display name, avatar) strictly to authenticate and publish videos on your behalf.

2. Data Usage: Tokens and media files are processed locally on our private server solely to publish posts requested by the account owner. We never sell or share data with third parties.

3. Revocation: You can revoke access anytime from TikTok Settings & Privacy > Security > Manage app permissions.
